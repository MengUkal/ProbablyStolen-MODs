━━━ 功能 ━━━
[list]

[hr]
[b]Fast Shutter[/b] by MengUkal
Speeds up the shutter animation on open/close and the closing sequence. Duration configurable in settings (vanilla is 2.75s); disabling the toggle restores vanilla feel. Minimal and dependency-free. Compatible with game 0.46.
