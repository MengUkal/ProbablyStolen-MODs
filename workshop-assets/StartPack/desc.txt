[b]New Game Scenarios[/b] by MengUkal
Adds 4 new starting scenarios (debt heir, black market outcast, collector, gambler) and 4 starting perks (seed money, supplier connections, old pal's toolbox, in-debt). Implemented purely with the official mod API. Compatible with game 0.46.
