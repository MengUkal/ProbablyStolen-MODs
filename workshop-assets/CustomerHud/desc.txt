[hr]
[b]Customer HUD[/b] by MengUkal
Shows the next customer's portrait, name and queue size at the top-right corner while open, with a "now serving" fallback. Includes a rebindable skip-customer hotkey. Coexists independently with the Profit Ledger stats bar. Compatible with game 0.46.
